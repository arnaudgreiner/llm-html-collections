How this sheet works

  1. Click a cell to select it — its row, column and box tint.
  2. Press 1–9 to fill, or 0 to erase.
  3. Hold N while pressing a number to scribble pencil marks in the corners.
  4. Check marks wrong entries in red; Reveal one writes in the true value.
  5. Arrow keys walk the grid. The timer runs until you reset.
