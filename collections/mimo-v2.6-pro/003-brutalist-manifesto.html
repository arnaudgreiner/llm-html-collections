01

Reject the smooth

Every edge declares its existence. We do not hide the seam, the border, the division between one thing and the next — we make it three pixels thick and we let it hold the page together.

RULE ONE
02

Type is structure

Letters are not decoration. At 150px they become architecture; at 11px they become data.

03

Colour with force

Three primaries. No tints. No gradients. Opacity is a compromise we refuse to make.

04

The grid shows

Columns are drawn, not implied. Twelve tracks, no exceptions, no mercy for the content.

WEIGHT IS HONEST.

06

Motion, not charm

Movement exists to inform: a ticker reports, a hover inverts, a button presses. Nothing floats.

07

Content over comfort

Whitespace is rationed like ink. If a region is empty, it is because the grid demanded it.

RULE SEVEN
08

Ship it raw

No polish pass. What you see is the artefact: markup, border, ink and nerve.
