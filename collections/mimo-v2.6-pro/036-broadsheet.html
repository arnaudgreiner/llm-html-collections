Council Votes to Keep the Clock Tower Clocks Wound by Hand

After a nine-month review, members rejected an electric movement — “the tick is part of the street”, one member said — and hired an apprentice instead.

The vote, taken shortly before eleven, was closer than anyone expected. Proponents of an electric movement had argued for reliability; opponents argued that a winding key turned once a week is the cheapest civic ritual the city still keeps.

The chamberlain estimated the saving at four hundred pounds a year. The clerk pointed out that the tower's bell is rung by a person, and that the two decisions are not unrelated.

An apprenticeship will be posted in the new year. Candidates must not be afraid of heights, or of Fridays.
